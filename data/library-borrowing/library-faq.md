# Library FAQ about borrowing, returning and renewing

3. Where can I find the rules, regulations, and procedures for borrowing/returning books at the library?

Please refer to the library policy on the library’s website: Library – VinUniversity

4. Do I have to pay to borrow books from the library?

No, you do not. You can borrow books from library for free.

5. How can I borrow library books to take home?

You can use the self – check machine on either the 1st floor or the 2nd floor to borrow or return print books. The 24/7 book return machine at the library main entrance is just for checking in books only.

Please click here to know how to borrow, return and renew books with self-check machines in the library.

7. What is the penalty for returning books late?

The library charges an overdue fine for late payment of the borrowed items from the due date or the expiration of the recall to the actual date of return and the date of notification of lost items from patrons. In addition, overdue fines always apply including holidays, vacations, and days off.

Fines are collected at the time incurred.

Normal material: 20,000 VND/ day overdue/ document.

Course-specific material: 20,000 VND/ hour overdue/ document.

Equipment: 20,000 VND/ hour overdue/ equipment.

* Note:

+ Failure to make a payment on time does not excuse patrons from the responsibility of returning or renewing items.

+ Patrons must pay both an overdue fine and an additional fee equivalent to the cover price of borrowed items if they have not been returned 30 days after the due date.

8. What should I do if I lose or damage a library book?

Lost or damaged items should be reported to the library in a timely manner.

Patrons will be charged fines for overdue materials/equipment or material/equipment repair and replacement fees. These fines and fees are regulated in VinUni’s Financial Regulations and Tariff.

Fees for repair or replacement based on the conditions of library items as follows:

9. What should I do if I discover that a borrowed document is torn, missing pages, or marked?

If you discover that a borrowed document is torn, missing pages, or marked, you must immediately report it to the library staff to avoid being held responsible for the missing pages. Provide them with specific details about the damage, such as the extent of tearing, the number of missing pages, or the nature of the markings. In your case, you may be subject to compensation as stipulated.

10. Where can I see the return date or overdue book return date?

To view information about borrowing books, due dates, or overdue status, you can visit the website Library – VinUniversity and select the ” My Library Account” section. Your login credentials will be your VinUni account.

Please read this libguides for more details.

11. After returning the book to the library, why do I still see it listed as overdue in my account?

First, please be sure that you returned the books at self-check machines or 24/7-return-station in library before bringing the books to the bookshelves.

Second, you need to check your email to see if there is any confirmation email from library about your return action.

Finally, please contact library staff directly at the Information Desk.

13. How to renew a borrowed print book?

Go to library home page at Library – VinUniversity

Log in with your VinUni ID (more log in tips can be found HERE )

Click RENEW ALL if you wish to renew all the borrowed books.

Or click LOAN and RENEW individual books.

TIPS: Please do the renewal one day just before the original due date to achieve the longest renewed due date.

Read this Libguide for more details: Request a print book for pick-up service/ Renew a print book – Books @ VinUni Library – LibGuides at Vin University

15. Can I borrow someone else’s library card to access the library?

No, you cannot. Sharing your VinUni library ID or library card with others is strictly prohibited.

19. I borrowed the book through the self-check machine, but the alarm sound still rang loudly from RFID gate, what should I do?

Please bring the book to Circulation desk at the 1st floor, library staff will support you.

22. Can I borrow library’s equipment for the long-time event outside of the library building?

No, you cannot. The library’s equipment can be used within 1 working day in the library only.

Exception: for some external events, you need confirmation from the event sponsor. Please inform your event sponsor to contact the library manager through email ([email protected],vn) for approval.
